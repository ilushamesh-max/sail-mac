Open the clipboard

Press Control + Shift + V from any app while Sail runs. You can also choose Clipboard in Sail or its menu bar.

Choose Enable history to allow new text copies from other apps. macOS may ask for separate clipboard permission. Existing clipboard text is skipped when history starts or resumes.

Sail keeps five distinct clips. Select one to edit a working copy. Save as new adds the edit to history. Copy text puts it on the system clipboard for you to paste.

Pause, revoke or clear

Pause history stops new capture and stays paused after restart. Revoke clipboard consent turns capture off until you explicitly enable it again. Both preserve existing clips. Delete or Clear history removes clips without changing the system clipboard.

Keep the latest five after quit saves text locally without added app encryption. Turn it off to remove the saved copy and keep clips only for the current session. Backups may retain older data. Source apps can omit privacy markers, so pause before copying passwords or other secrets.

Use DROPZONEZ

Choose DROPZONEZ in the file wheel to hold file or folder references. Press Control + Shift + D to open the box. Holding or removing references leaves originals in place.

Select the references you need. Choose Move, a favorite destination, or drag Move out to an ordinary Finder folder. The move changes the originals’ location. Canceled or failed moves stay held. Temporary, cache, app and container destinations are refused by Move out.

Move and undo

  1. Choose Move in Sail, or File > Move files or folders.
  2. Select originals, then their destination parent.
  3. Confirm Move here. Cancel leaves originals in place.

Duplicate names get a number. Undo restores exact original paths when access is available and those paths are free. After restart, Sail may ask you to choose the exact original parent folder or moved item again. Cancel leaves the recovery record intact.

If Sail reports an uncertain outcome, inspect both shown locations before trying another move. Undo never overwrites an occupied original path.

Choose an app or make an image copy

Use Open With in the wheel, or File > Open files with app. Choose a compatible local Mac app or use Choose App. This does not change the default app. The chosen app may edit or transmit files under its own practices.

Images opens PNG, JPEG, HEIC and resize options. Sail creates copies, keeps proportions without enlarging smaller images, and leaves originals unchanged. Results use 8-bit sRGB and omit source photo metadata.

Keep Sail ready

Closing a window leaves Sail running in the menu bar. Enable Start at login in Settings if you want it ready after sign-in with its windows closed. Start at login is optional.

Command-Q and Quit Sail ask for confirmation. Keep Running is the default. Choose Quit Sail to stop the app and its shortcuts. System logout and restart do not show the extra quit confirmation.

Send a useful support request

Include Sail’s version, your macOS version, the action and exact error. Remove private text and paths from screenshots. Do not send passwords or private documents.
