Sail is published by Eliyahu Mechtcheriakov. This notice covers the Mac app and its website. Updated 25 October 2026.

App data

Sail has no account, advertising or file-upload service. This release does not collect or send usage analytics. It does not send file contents or filenames to a developer server.

Preferences on your Mac store image settings, chosen folders, DROPZONEZ references, favorite-folder bookmarks, file identifiers and move/Undo records. Holding or removing references does not copy, move or delete their contents.

Clipboard consent and capture

Clipboard history starts off, including after an upgrade that has no consent record. Choosing Enable history gives Sail permission to observe new text copies from other apps while it runs, including in the background. macOS controls separate clipboard access. Refusing it does not enable a bypass.

The menu bar recording symbol and text-bank status indicate capture. Pause stops it and is remembered after restart. Revoke clipboard consent stops it until you explicitly enable history again. Existing clips remain until deleted or cleared. Launch and resume skip text already on the clipboard. Sail does not log keystrokes or paste automatically into other apps.

Retention and deletion

Keep the latest five after quit is selected by default in the consent view and can be turned off before enabling. Retained text and copy timestamps are stored in local app preferences without added app encryption. Turning retention off removes that saved copy and keeps the current session. Unsaved edits are session-only.

Delete removes a selected clip. Clear history removes session and retained clips, including unsaved drafts. The system clipboard stays unchanged. System or device backups may include older saved text.

History is limited to five distinct clips, 64 KB per clip and 320 KB of text in total. Repeat copies move to the top. Sail skips text marked concealed, transient, generated or protected by its source. Apps may omit these markers, so filtering cannot exclude every secret. Pause before copying sensitive information.

Copy text puts the editor’s text on the system clipboard for your next paste. Other software with clipboard access can read it.

File and image actions

Move and accepted Move out drags change original locations after the chosen destination is confirmed or accepted. Undo checks saved file identities and may require renewed folder access. Image conversion creates copies, preserves originals and omits source photo metadata.

Copy Path writes full paths to the system clipboard. Open With hands selected files to a Mac app you choose without changing the default app. Share opens the macOS sharing picker. The app or sharing service you choose may process, retain or transmit files under its own practices.

When enabled, the Finder wheel observes mouse drags and the Shift modifier using macOS APIs. It does not log typing or record the screen. Global shortcuts register specific key combinations rather than recording keystrokes. Start at login runs only when enabled and can be disabled in Sail or macOS Login Items.

Usage data

This release does not collect feature-use analytics, create analytics identifiers or send app activity to an analytics service. Review-request eligibility counters stay on your Mac.

Review requests

After repeated successful use on different days, Sail may request an App Store review during a quiet visit to its main window. Apple controls whether the native prompt appears. Sail cannot read your rating or review. Eligibility counters stay on your Mac, independent of analytics consent.

This website and support

This static site uses no cookies, analytics, forms, accounts or browser storage. The email link opens your mail app. If you send a support email, its content and address are handled through the email service so we can reply. Avoid private documents and sensitive clipboard text.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection. The site adds no cookies, analytics or advertising.
